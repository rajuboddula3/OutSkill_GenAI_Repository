DATA DYNAMICS - ENGINEERING ONBOARDING GUIDE
Document ID: DD-ENG-001
Owner: Anya Raghavan, Head of Data Engineering
Last revised: 12 January 2026
Audience: All new engineering hires (Data Engineer, Analytics Engineer, ML Engineer)

1. YOUR FIRST DAY

Welcome to Data Dynamics. Your first day is deliberately light on delivery work. IT will hand you a
company laptop that has already been enrolled in mobile device management. Do not install a personal
machine for client work; our client contracts forbid processing customer data on unmanaged hardware.

Before lunch you should have completed three things: signed the confidentiality acknowledgement in
Workday, enabled multi-factor authentication on your Okta account, and joined the #dd-engineering
channel in Slack. Your buddy - assigned to you by your team lead - will walk you through the rest.

2. GETTING ACCESS TO SYSTEMS

Access at Data Dynamics is request-based, never default. Every system is fronted by an Okta group and
you request membership through the Access Portal. Requests are approved by the system owner, not by
your manager, so expect approvals to arrive out of order.

The standard bundle for a new data engineer is:
  - okta-grp-warehouse-reader        (read access to the Snowflake analytics warehouse)
  - okta-grp-airflow-dev             (author and trigger DAGs in the development environment)
  - okta-grp-github-dd               (source control, read-write on non-protected branches)
  - okta-grp-vpn-standard            (network access to internal services)

Production write access is deliberately excluded from the starter bundle. It is granted only after you
have shipped two changes to development and completed the Data Handling course described in section 5.
Requests for production write access must be approved by both the platform owner and the security
team, and are reviewed quarterly. Expect five to seven working days for the full approval chain.

VPN is required for anything that is not Slack, Workday or GitHub. The VPN client is Tailscale; your
device is authorised automatically once okta-grp-vpn-standard is approved. If a service times out,
check the VPN before filing a ticket - roughly a third of first-week tickets are VPN related.

3. LOCAL DEVELOPMENT ENVIRONMENT

We standardise on Python 3.12 and manage environments with uv. Clone the platform monorepo, run
"uv sync" inside the package you are working on, and use "uv run" rather than activating a virtual
environment by hand. This keeps the lockfile authoritative and avoids the drift that used to cause
"works on my machine" failures.

Docker Desktop is licensed for all engineers and is required to run the local Postgres and MinIO
containers that back the integration tests. Allocate at least 8 GB of memory to Docker or the test
suite will be killed by the out-of-memory reaper partway through.

Secrets never live in the repository. Local development reads credentials from a .env file that is
generated for you by running "make dev-secrets"; the file is git-ignored and rotates every 30 days.
If you find a hardcoded credential in any repository, treat it as a security incident and follow the
reporting procedure in the security policy document.

4. HOW WE WORK

Sprints are two weeks and start on a Wednesday. Standup is asynchronous and posted in the team channel
by 10:00 local time. We keep a written record of decisions: anything that changes an interface, a
schema or a service-level objective needs a short design note in Notion before implementation starts.

Every change reaches production through a pull request. Two approvals are required for changes that
touch production pipelines, one approval for everything else. CI must be green; we do not merge on red
with a promise to fix forward. The on-call engineer for the week has the authority to block any merge
that would land during a change freeze.

5. REQUIRED TRAINING

Three courses must be completed within your first 30 days:
  - Data Handling and Client Confidentiality (90 minutes, annual refresher)
  - Incident Response Basics (60 minutes, one-off)
  - Introduction to the Data Dynamics Platform (self-paced, roughly 4 hours)

The Data Handling course is a prerequisite for production access, so schedule it in week one rather
than leaving it to day 29. Completion is tracked automatically; your access request will simply sit in
a pending state until the course record appears.

6. WHO TO ASK

Platform and pipeline questions go to #dd-platform. Warehouse modelling and dbt questions go to
#dd-analytics. Anything involving client data classification goes to Clara Mendez, our Data Protection
Officer, rather than to a channel. If you are blocked for more than half a day, escalate to your team
lead - we would much rather unblock you early than discover it at the sprint review.
